Preparing an application

Auto mode
Approve drafted free-text answers without asking, and mark the application ready. It does not submit automatically; the separate send action requires your click.
Tailor the résumé per posting
Drops and rewords bullets from your master to suit the job, then attaches that PDF instead. A reworded bullet that introduces a number or a technology your résumé never mentioned is rejected and the original kept.
Draft a cover letter per posting
Written from the posting, your résumé and your notes, into the same approval gate the form answers use.
